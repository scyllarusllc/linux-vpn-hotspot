Wi-Fi card check

The hotspot needs two Wi-Fi cards. This checks every step between the USB plug and NetworkManager: is the adapter on the USB bus, did a driver take it, what the kernel said about it, is Wi-Fi switched off, and does NetworkManager use the card. Nothing is changed unless you press a fix button.
